0 – 200 m · Sunlight Zone

The ocean begins where the sky ends.

Sunlight reaches this far and no further. Nearly every wave you've ever seen breaks here, in the thin, bright skin of a much deeper world.

200 – 1,000 m · Twilight Zone

Light fades to a memory here.

Only a blue haze survives this deep, too faint for anything to grow by. Many creatures rise toward the surface each night and sink back before dawn.

1,000 – 4,000 m · Midnight Zone

Past this line, the sun has never once mattered.

In total darkness, most animals make their own light — a flash to startle, a lure to tempt, a glow to say not prey, not food, not you.

4,000 – 6,000 m · Abyssal Zone

Three quarters of the seafloor lies down here.

Near freezing, crushed under hundreds of atmospheres, this is the ocean's quiet majority — flat plains of sediment settled over millions of years.

6,000 – 10,935 m · Hadal Zone

You've reached the Challenger Deep.

Deeper than Everest is tall, this trench has been visited by fewer people than the surface of the moon. There is nowhere further down to go.
